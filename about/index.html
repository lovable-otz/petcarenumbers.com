About Pet Care Numbers

Pet Care Numbers is a free set of dog and cat calculators — pet age, daily calories and food portions, pregnancy due dates, chocolate exposure, pet insurance reimbursement and the yearly cost of owning a pet — that apply formulas and figures from the Merck Veterinary Manual, AAHA/AAFP guidelines, International Cat Care, NAPHIA and the ASPCA, and name the source under every result.

Pet Care Numbers is a set of free calculators for dog and cat owners. They cover a pet’s age in human years, daily calories and portions, a puppy’s breed-average adult weight, pregnancy due dates, the dose in a chocolate ingestion, pet insurance and yearly costs. The site is maintained by Pet Care Numbers.

Each tool is built on a named source: the Merck Veterinary Manual, AAHA and AAHA/AAFP guidelines, International Cat Care, the Ohio State University Veterinary Medical Center, published research, NAPHIA’s industry report and the ASPCA’s cost table. Every formula is on the methodology page.

This site is not a veterinary practice, and nobody here examines or treats animals. The calculators give estimates for planning, not a diagnosis, treatment or feeding prescription. The site publishes no medication doses. If you think your pet has eaten something harmful, call your veterinarian, the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at (888) 426-4435 or the Pet Poison Helpline at (855) 764-7661.

The site does not sell insurance or recommend any insurer. At launch there is no advertising, and there are no affiliate or lead-generation partners.

Corrections. If you find a figure that is wrong or out of date, or a result that does not match its stated formula, please report it through the contact page. Reports are checked against the original source. Confirmed errors are corrected promptly, and the page’s update date changes when a result is affected. NAPHIA premiums are reviewed each June, and the other sources at least once a year.
