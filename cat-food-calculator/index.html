Cat Food Calculator

A 10 lb spayed or neutered cat needs about 261 calories a day, which is 1.2 × its resting energy requirement in the Merck Veterinary Manual’s table. This cat food calculator works out that figure from your cat’s weight and life stage, then uses your food’s label to turn it into cups of dry food or cans of wet food.

Updated September 20, 2026 · By Pet Care Numbers

How many calories does a cat need per day?

Multiply the resting energy requirement (RER = 70 × weight in kg^0.75) by a life-stage factor. Merck (updated September 2024) uses 1.2 for neutered adults, 1.4 for intact adults, 1.0 for cats that gain weight easily and 2.5 for kittens.

A 10 lb cat weighs 4.54 kg, so its RER is about 218 kcal. The factors give these daily totals for a 10 lb cat:

10 lb catFactor × RERkcal a day
Neutered adult1.2261
Intact adult1.4305
Gains weight easily1.0218

Merck calls every calorie calculation a starting point that may need changing depending on how the cat responds. It adds that one animal can need up to 30% more or fewer calories than another of the same weight. Ohio State’s Veterinary Medical Center puts the possible difference at up to 50%. It advises adjusting the amount up or down to keep a healthy body condition score.

How much wet or dry food should I feed my cat?

Divide the calories by the kcal per cup or per can printed on the label. For the 10 lb neutered cat, the calculator keeps 10% of the 261 kcal for treats and feeds the other 235 kcal. With a dry food of 400 kcal per cup, that is 0.59 cups a day, or about 0.29 cups per meal over two meals.

Wet food works the same way. A can’s calorie content is on its label, so enter it and choose “can.” If you feed both kinds, decide how many calories each one should supply, then run the calculator once for each food with that share of the total.

Labels differ a lot from one food to the next, so a “half cup” of one food is not the same as a half cup of another. Measure with a proper 8 oz measuring cup, or weigh the food on a kitchen scale if the label gives kcal per kilogram. The 10% treat allowance comes from the 2021 AAHA Nutrition and Weight Management Guidelines.

How much should a kitten eat?

Merck uses 2.5 × RER for kittens, and notes that kittens can also be fed free choice. A 4 kg kitten comes out at about 495 kcal a day. With a wet food of 90 kcal per can, that is 5.5 cans a day, split across several meals.

Kittens should eat a diet made for growth. The AAHA guidelines (J Am Anim Hosp Assoc 2021;57:153–174) say that for cats, this usually means feeding a growth diet until about 1 year of age. International Cat Care also recommends a growth-type diet for a pregnant cat, continuing until her kittens are weaned.

A kitten’s weight changes quickly, so recalculate every week or two. Ask your veterinarian to confirm the portions for any kitten, and for a pregnant or nursing queen, whose needs are outside this tool’s factors. The cat age calculator shows when your kitten moves into the young-adult stage.

How do I tell if my cat is at a healthy weight?

Veterinary teams use a body condition score (BCS), a hands-on check of body fat. The 2021 AAHA guidelines recommend the 9-point scale and suggest scoring at every exam. A score below 4 or above 5 on that scale calls for a closer look.

AAHA links each step up the scale to about 5% more body fat. Each point above 5 means the cat is roughly 10% overweight. The guidelines recommend that the practice team teach owners to score their own pets, and that a team member checks the owner’s score.

AAHA also notes that some breeds are prone to weight gain, and that weight tends to rise through middle age. Weigh your cat every few weeks. If the weight or body shape changes, talk to your veterinarian before changing the food. For a cat that needs to lose weight, your vet sets the target and the plan. Budget for those checkups with the vet cost calculator. Merck notes that a special weight-loss diet may be needed when calories are cut hard, to prevent nutrient deficiencies.

Frequently asked questions

Is 2/3 cup of food enough for a 10-pound cat all day?

It depends on the food’s calories. A 10 lb neutered cat needs about 261 kcal a day. With a 400 kcal/cup dry food, 2/3 cup supplies about 267 kcal, which is just over that and leaves no room for treats. With a denser food, the same scoop is more. Check the label’s kcal per cup.

Is 1/2 cup of food a day enough for a cat?

For a 10 lb neutered cat eating 400 kcal/cup food, half a cup gives about 200 kcal. That is less than the formula’s 261 kcal and a little under the 218 kcal for a cat that gains weight easily. Individual needs vary, so ask your vet and watch your cat’s body condition.

How much should an indoor cat eat per day?

Merck’s factors don’t include an indoor category. Most pet cats are neutered, so the calculator starts at 1.2 × RER, about 261 kcal for a 10 lb cat. For a cat that gains weight easily, 1.0 × RER is about 218 kcal. Adjust by body condition with your vet.

How can I work out a daily feeding amount for my cat by weight?

Enter your cat’s weight and life stage above. The calculator applies RER × the Merck factor, then divides by the calories on your food label. Change the weight to see the whole range. Merck says the result is a starting point, not a fixed ration.
