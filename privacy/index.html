Privacy policy

Last updated September 20, 2026.

What you type into our tools is processed in your browser and is not sent to us. The tools do not store your entries. If you press “Copy link to this result”, your entries are placed in the copied link after the # sign so the person you share it with sees the same result; that part of a link is not sent to our server, and our analytics is set up to ignore it.

Analytics, advertising and partners

Analytics. This site uses Google Analytics 4 to see, in aggregate, how visitors use it: which pages people view, how they arrive and what kinds of devices they use. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar technologies. It collects information such as the pages you visit, your browser and device type, and your approximate location. You can stop Google Analytics from recording your visits with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, which is available for Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge, or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

What you type into the calculators. Calculator inputs, such as your pet’s age, weight and breed, a breeding date, the chocolate eaten, insurance quotes and costs, are processed in your browser. They are not sent to this site or stored by it. They leave your browser only if you press “Copy link.” That button creates a link containing your current inputs so you can return to them or share them. Anyone you send the link to can see those inputs.

Advertising and partners. At launch this site shows no advertising, has no pet insurance, pet food, affiliate or lead-generation partners, and does not sell personal information. Before any advertising network or partner is added, this policy will be updated to name it, describe what it collects and explain how to opt out.
